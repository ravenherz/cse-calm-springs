# Calm Springs Engine

Your site. Your archive. Your look. No platform in the middle.

CSE is the engine behind [ravenherz.com](https://www.ravenherz.com/) — a personal publishing system for someone who wants a real home on the web, not a profile on someone else’s feed.

Write pages. Hang a gallery from a folder of photos. Put a record on the same site. Change the skin when the decade changes. Keep the keys.

## Why it exists

Most “personal sites” are rented: a theme store, a plugin maze, an algorithm, a bill. CSE is the opposite. One operator. One place. Content you actually own.

It has been running, in one form or another, since 2017. That is not a pitch-deck story. It is a site that had to keep working.

## What you get

**A homepage that behaves like a portfolio**  
Categories become sections. You decide what is in the nav, what stays quiet, how much of each collection shows on the first screen, and which piece opens the page.

**Pages and albums**  
A page is a story with a stable address — title, cover, tags, comments. Markdown when you want speed. HTML when you want control. An album is a gallery: pick a folder of pictures, and the public site shows them. Captions travel with the files.

**Media without the ritual**  
Drop in a photo — including HEIC from an iPhone — and it is ready for the web. Folders nest the way a studio actually files things (`Travel / 2024 / Iceland`). Large images get a light preview so the site stays fast. Audio keeps title, artist, and artwork from the file.

**Music that belongs on the site**  
Build a playlist once. Embed it in any page. The player is part of the house, not a widget rented from somewhere else.

**Looks you can change without moving the furniture**  
A 2000s card feed. A modern studio with several color rooms. A JavaScript shell that reads the same site. An exhibition hall. Switch the skin; the archive stays.

**A catalog, not a dashboard circus**  
One tree for writing, galleries, music, files, small apps, and skins. Create and edit in the same desk. The public site stays the public site.

**Room for small apps**  
Ship a self-contained piece and it sits beside the rest of the site — experiments, tools, extras — without a second hosting story.

**You hold the switch**  
Accounts, who can read, who can edit. A first-boot setup that turns an empty box into a site. An archive you can take with you. Settings you change without waiting on a vendor. The live site is the product.

## Who it is for

Makers who want one address for writing, pictures, and sound. People who still care how a homepage feels. Anyone tired of looking like every other template.

If you need a thousand authors, a plugin marketplace, and a growth funnel — this is not that. If you want a house instead of a listing, it is.
